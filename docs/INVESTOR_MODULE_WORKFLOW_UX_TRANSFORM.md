# Investor Discovery, Risk Review, Funding Readiness, and Opportunity Tracking -- Workflow-First UX Transformation

**Module:** Investor / Capital Deployment Workspace  
**Date:** 2026-05-17  
**Status:** Design Blueprint -- Ready for Review

---

# 1. MODULE EXPERIENCE VISION

## What the User Is Trying to Accomplish

An investor is trying to **find opportunities worth capital, understand the risk, and decide whether the opportunity is ready for financing, co-investment, or project progression**.

The module should guide evaluation, not just display assets.

## Emotional / Operational State

| Actor | Emotional State | Operational State |
|---|---|---|
| **New Investor** | Curious, risk-aware | Needs a clear path to assess opportunities |
| **Active Investor** | Analytical, comparison-heavy | Tracks multiple assets and readiness states |
| **Reviewing Investor** | Decisive, deadline-driven | Needs evidence, timeline, and blockers |

## What the Ideal Workflow Should Feel Like

> "The system tells me whether this opportunity is worth deeper review, what is missing, and what I should do next."

---

# 2. USER GOALS

### Primary Goals
1. Discover viable opportunities
2. Evaluate readiness and risk
3. Track funding or project paths
4. Decide whether to proceed

### Urgent Goals
5. Identify blockers
6. Watch deadlines and approvals
7. Exit weak opportunities quickly

---

# 3. WORKFLOW-FIRST UX REDESIGN

| Stage | What User Sees | Main CTA | Secondary Actions | Blockers | Guidance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | filtered opportunity cards | Open opportunity | shortlist | incomplete data | recommend higher-signal assets |
| Review | property readiness and financial signals | Request further review | compare | missing documents | show what is missing |
| Decision | summary decision panel | Proceed | save for later | risk threshold exceeded | explain risk clearly |
| Funding | investment or finance workflow | Submit intent | contact owner | funding shortfall | explain next requirement |
| Tracking | live opportunity timeline | View progress | archive | stalled response | surface escalation path |

---

# 4. FINAL UX TRANSFORMATION SUMMARY

The investor workspace should help the user eliminate weak options fast and focus on opportunities that are structurally ready.
