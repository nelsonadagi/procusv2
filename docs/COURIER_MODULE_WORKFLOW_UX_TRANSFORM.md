# Courier Pickup, Pricing, Tracking, and Delivery Completion -- Workflow-First UX Transformation

**Module:** Courier / Delivery Workspace  
**Date:** 2026-05-17  
**Status:** Design Blueprint -- Ready for Review

---

# 1. MODULE EXPERIENCE VISION

## What the User Is Trying to Accomplish

A courier is trying to **accept delivery work, move items reliably, and close the delivery without confusion**.

The module should feel like a live route and execution assistant.

---

# 2. USER GOALS

### Primary Goals
1. Accept delivery jobs
2. See the next pickup or drop-off
3. Update pricing and availability
4. Complete delivery cleanly

### Urgent Goals
5. Respond to a new shipment
6. Resolve a failed delivery
7. Confirm proof of delivery

---

# 3. WORKFLOW-FIRST UX REDESIGN

| Stage | What User Sees | Main CTA | Secondary Actions | Blockers | Guidance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ready | available jobs | Accept job | update pricing | unverified profile | show readiness checklist |
| In Transit | route and status | Mark arrived | call recipient | missing assignment data | keep the next step visible |
| Delivery Attempt | delivery details | Confirm delivery | upload proof | recipient unavailable | show recovery path |
| Complete | finished shipment | Close job | review earnings | none | summarize outcome |

---

# 4. FINAL UX TRANSFORMATION SUMMARY

The courier workspace should reduce uncertainty around the next stop, the next proof step, and the next closure action.
