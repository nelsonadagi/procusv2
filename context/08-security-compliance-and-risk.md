# Security, Compliance, And Risk

## Security themes

The platform documentation expects security to be embedded into normal workflow handling rather than bolted on later.

## Core concerns

- authenticated access
- role-based authorization
- administrative controls
- audit logging
- request throttling
- dispute-safe financial handling
- compliance-driven onboarding
- data protection for regulated actors

## Compliance themes

- KYC
- AML
- jurisdiction-specific rules
- public procurement traceability
- regulatory reporting
- enterprise governance

## Risk and intelligence themes

- contractor or entity scoring
- AI-assisted risk prediction
- suspicious activity or policy alerts
- reporting automation

## Practical guidance

When implementing changes in this platform, evaluate:

- who can perform the action
- what role or permission is required
- whether the action affects money, trust, or legal state
- whether audit logging is needed
- whether throttling, verification, or hold logic should apply
