# Phases And Domains

## Delivery model

The documentation describes the product as a six-phase platform. Each phase expands the domain boundary rather than replacing earlier work.

## Phase 1: Materials Liquidity Engine

Focus:

- accounts
- vendors
- catalog
- orders
- payments
- disputes
- reviews
- taxonomy

Outcome:

- a construction materials marketplace with quote-to-order foundations

## Phase 2: Contracts And Execution Layer

Focus:

- contractors
- contracts
- bids
- milestones

Outcome:

- tender posting, contractor discovery, bidding, award, and execution checkpoints

## Phase 3: Financial Infrastructure Layer

Focus:

- escrow
- finance
- scoring

Outcome:

- escrow-backed releases, disputes, and early embedded finance capability

## Phase 4: Projects, Property, And Capital

Focus:

- projects
- property
- investments

Outcome:

- top-level project objects, project requirements, commitments, property and capital workflows

## Phase 5: Regulated Investment And Government

Focus:

- regulation
- enterprise
- government
- compliance

Outcome:

- KYC/AML, public tendering, enterprise-grade workflows, regulated investment patterns

## Phase 6: Intelligence And Scale

Focus:

- banking
- reporting
- ai_engine
- liquidity
- integrations
- risk

Outcome:

- settlement, regulatory reporting, AI prediction, secondary marketplace concepts, ERP integration
